# **UI Concept - Bloom Tracker**

## **Purpose**
Give information on when plants and trees will bloom

## **Users**
- Observers: want to find blooming plants and learn more about them
- Contributors: submit photos to add new trees and plants or identify existing photos

## **Core Screens**
- Map marking flowering plant locations
- Details of a specific plant
- Plant submission

## **Actions**
- Submit plant for tracking
- Help identify plant and blooming periods
- Select map marker to bring up more details
